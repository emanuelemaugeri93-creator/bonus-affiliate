How to use this tool (click to hide / show)
  1. Route - choose one of 6 ready-made trade routes (or Blank), then drag cities, add roads, and set what each town sells cheaply or wants badly.
  2. Goods - 4 to 16 goods with prices, volatility, weight, spoilage and an icon.
  3. Events - road dangers, customs, festivals and more. Edit the choices and test-fire any event.
  4. Rules - cash, capacity, game length, goal, difficulty, upgrades, texts and one of 6 themes (live preview on the right).
  5. Balance - the simulator plays 1,000 games with three bots and tells you in plain English if the game is too easy, too hard or has a money loop. One click fixes it.
  6. Export - play-test, then download one self-contained .html game.

Period details are historically inspired and simplified; names are real places where fitting, everything else is invented. Your work autosaves in this browser.
