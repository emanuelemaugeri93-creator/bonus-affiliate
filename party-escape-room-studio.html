    How to use Party Escape Room Studio

    1

    Pick the story

    Choose a theme, the audience, difficulty, time limit and the lock you will buy. Every theme has a full intro, goal and ending.

    2

    Five stations, one lock

    Cipher, number lock, hidden word, letter maze and pattern. Each gives one token. A final puzzle turns the five tokens into the lock code, and the code is checked by the app.

    3

    Content Lock and your image

    Name your own hiding places, add secret words and a story title. Upload your invitation to print with matching colours.

    4

    Print and host

    Player View shows the printable kit. Host View shows the host guide and answer key. Export one PDF for the kit plus answers, and the host guide as its own PDF. The Host Timer works offline.

    5

    Shortcuts

    G generate, A player/host view, T host timer, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
