How to use Claim Review Scanner

This tool FLAGS sentences that deserve a human check. It never says a statement is true or false, and it does not verify anything. You, the author, remain responsible for the accuracy and safety of your book.

1. Quick start

  1. Click Manuscript. A sample chapter with deliberate flags is already loaded so you can try the tool. Click Load sample chapter to bring it back.
  2. Replace it with your text: paste a chapter or the whole book, or upload a .txt or .md file. Word (.docx) files are not read: copy the text out of Word and paste it.
  3. Click Analyze. Large books (100,000 words) take a few seconds and show a progress bar.
  4. Work through the Findings list. Click a finding to jump to it in the text, check it against a reliable source, then set its status.
  5. When you are done, open Export for the PDF report, CSV, source ledger and checklist.

2. What gets flagged

  • Numbers with units: temperatures, times and durations in food, water or storage contexts, pressures, electrical values (volts, amps, watts, wire gauge, breakers, grounding), water-treatment amounts (bleach, drops per gallon, ppm, micron ratings), quantities and conversions.
  • Risky words: food preservation (canning, botulism, curing), absolute promises (always, never, guaranteed, 100%), medical and legal language.
  • Off-niche topics: weapons, tactical, wilderness survival and medical treatment are flagged as "Off-niche: consider removing".
  • Consistency: when the same topic has different values in different places (for example "1 gallon per person" and "2 gallons per person"), both sentences are flagged.
  • Severity is High, Medium or Low and is always written as a label ([H], [M], [L]), never by color alone. A highlight does not mean the text is wrong. It means a person should check it.

3. Reviewing a finding

Select a finding to see why it was flagged and what to verify. Set a status: To verify, Verified, Rewritten, Accepted as is or Removed. Add a note, and attach a source from your ledger or add a new one. Use the filters (severity, category, status, chapter, search) and Apply to category to change many findings at once. The tiles and the chapter summary show your progress.

Extra tools

  • Flag map: the narrow strip beside the text shows where flags are (black = high, striped = medium, dotted = low). Click it to jump.
  • Risk by chapter: unresolved flags weighted by severity per 1,000 words, written as Low, Medium or High. It is a guide for where to look first, not a quality score.
  • Possible rewrite approach: for absolute promises, medical wording and off-niche passages the detail box suggests how to rewrite. These are prompts, not facts.
  • Ignore this sentence: hides a false alarm in all future scans (Rules shows the count and can clear it).
  • Numbers: a table of every number next to a topic word, with conflicts marked. Export it as CSV.
  • Disclaimer starter: Export builds a draft disclaimer from the risk areas found in your text. It is a starting point only and should be reviewed by a qualified professional.

Editing text

  • Edit this sentence (in the detail box, or double-click a highlighted sentence) rewrites that sentence in your manuscript, marks it Rewritten and scans again.
  • Report text in Export lets you change the report title, add "Prepared by" and a cover note that are printed on the PDF cover.

4. Rules

Open Rules to switch rules on or off, change severity, edit a pattern, or add your own. Patterns are regular expressions. If a pattern is invalid it is shown in red and skipped. Reset rules restores the built-in list.

5. Source ledger

Record where you checked each fact: title, link, date accessed and type (official agency, university extension, manufacturer, book, other). One source can support many findings.

6. Export

  • Review report (PDF): cover summary, key numbers, findings table with status and source, ledger.
  • Findings (CSV) for spreadsheets. Source ledger (Markdown). Clean checklist (TXT) of what is still unresolved.
  • Save project stores rules, statuses, notes, ledger and the manuscript text in one .json file. Your browser also autosaves rules, statuses, notes and sources. The manuscript text is kept only if you tick "Remember manuscript".

7. Troubleshooting

  • Nothing is flagged: check that the text was analyzed and that rules are switched on.
  • Too many flags: switch off low-value rules or filter by severity.
  • A rule is red: its pattern is not a valid regular expression. Fix or switch it off.
  • PDF fails: the PDF library needs an internet connection the first time the page loads.

8. Shortcuts

J/K next/previous finding, V verified, R rewritten, A accepted as is, X removed, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Y redo, F1 this guide, Esc close.
