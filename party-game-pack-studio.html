How to use Party Game Pack Studio

1

Enter the event

Choose the occasion, type one or two names, the date and the venue, pick a theme palette and press Generate (or key G). Every game is built and checked by code.

2

Choose your games

Switch bingo, word search, quiz, scramble, matching and the sign and card set on or off, and set how many cards, words and questions you want. Each game gets a puzzle page and an answer page.

3

Content Lock

Add up to 30 personal quiz facts (question | answer), your own word list and your own bingo squares. The generator uses exactly what you type.

4

Pick a trim

In KDP Trim Studio choose 6 x 9, 8 x 10, 8.5 x 11, A4, or the 5 x 7 and 4 x 6 card sizes. Turn bleed on if needed and show the safe-area guides. Everything resizes to fit.

5

Style and brand

Choose a preset (use Print-safe B/W for black and white printing), edit the colors and add your own "Created by" line for your shop.

6

Export and save

Download Exact PDF builds one PDF with the cover, how-to page, games and answers, numbered correctly. PNG ZIP and single-page SVG are in Export Deck. Undo / Redo, autosave, Save .json and the short seed code protect your work.

7

Shortcuts

G generate · A toggle answer view · Left / Right change page · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo · H this help.
