How to use Metro Mystery

1

Generate your city

Set the puzzle count (1-40), difficulty and theme pack - or paste your own station names with Custom. Press Generate. Every map is checked for valid interchanges, and every question has a computed, unique answer.

2

Custom names + AI prompt

Paste 40+ names (comma or line separated) into the Custom box. Copy AI prompt copies a ready-made prompt that asks any AI chat for 60 themed station names - the app never calls an AI itself.

3

Style it live

Pick a preset, map style, legend toggle and answer-key style. Every control updates the preview instantly. In Print-safe B/W each line gets its own dash pattern plus numbered end badges.

4

Preview and export

Toggle Puzzle / Answer View - answers draw each route on its own highlighted map. Download Exact PDF renders puzzles first, then the key; Exact Print gives one page per sheet.

5

Tips

A stop is one station hop; a change is one line transfer. The seed in the footer reproduces that exact city.
