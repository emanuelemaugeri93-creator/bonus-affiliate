    How to use Clipart to Coloring Page Studio

    1

    Add your clipart

    Drop PNG, JPG or WebP files on the Convert panel, or press Add Starter Set to try it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Everything runs in this browser.

    2

    Pick a mode

    Silhouette Outline traces the edge. Detail Lines finds the edges inside the picture. Color Regions merges the colors and draws lines between them, which suits soft watercolor art. Soft or very low-contrast art gives rougher lines.

    3

    Choose a detail level

    Easy gives thick lines and few details for kids. Detailed gives fine lines for adults. Fine-tune with the sliders and read the hints under the preview. Any image can have its own settings.

    4

    Lay out the book

    Choose images per page, a frame, titles, a color guide and your brand details. Pick the KDP trim. The blue box is the safe area and the red line is the inside margin. Guides never print.

    5

    Export

    Press Convert all, then download the Exact PDF, the PNG ZIP, or the line art only ZIP. The Print or save as PDF button at the top works too.

    6

    Rights

    Convert only artwork you own or have the right to use, and check the rules of the marketplace where you sell before you publish.

    7

    Shortcuts

    G convert all, C compare, ← → pages, + - zoom, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+P print, H help.
