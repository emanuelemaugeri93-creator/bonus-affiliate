How to use Preparedness Plan Calculator Pages

These are planning aids with editable assumptions, not professional advice. Every default can be changed, and the pages contain numbers and fill-in fields only, no safety, medical or treatment instructions.

1. Quick start

  1. Pick a tab (Water, Food, Pantry Rotation, Power, Budget, Household Plan) and type your own numbers. Click "Use the example numbers" to see a worked example.
  2. The result tiles update as you type. The worksheet page on the right updates too.
  3. Open Book and Export to choose the style, trim size and which pages go in your book. Each calculator has a Blank worksheet, a Filled page and a Worked example page.
  4. Download Exact PDF or a PNG ZIP, or use Exact Print.

2. The calculators

  • Water: people, optional pets, days, amount per person per day (gallons or liters), optional hygiene extra, and container sizes with the count needed and a storage footprint you enter.
  • Food: calorie target for each member, days, and a table of stored foods. Shows total calories, days covered, shortfall and coverage with a bar chart.
  • Pantry Rotation: items with quantity, location and best-by date. Sorted by date with "use first" order, expired and soon-to-expire flags, a month-by-month timeline and printable first-in-first-out shelf labels.
  • Power: devices (watts, hours a day), days of autonomy, depth of discharge (default 50%), efficiency (default 85%) and peak sun hours (default 4). Gives daily watt-hours, battery capacity and solar size. This is a planning estimate, not an electrical design.
  • Budget: categories with planned and actual monthly amounts, totals and a 12-month plan.
  • Household Plan: fill-in fields for contacts, meeting places, a document checklist and shut-off locations. Blank or filled. No procedures.

3. Rounding and input rules

  • Numbers must be 0 or more. Text, negatives and empty values are rejected with a message and the previous valid value is kept.
  • Counts of people, pets and days are whole numbers. Container counts and battery and solar sizes are rounded up. Result volumes and Ah show 1 decimal, assumption amounts 2 decimals, calories and watt-hours whole numbers, money 2 decimals.
  • Zero people or zero days is allowed and gives zero needs.
  • Pantry dates use full dates. A date before "today" is flagged Expired, within 30 days is flagged Soon. Items with no date go last.

4. Worksheet pages

Long tables continue on extra pages automatically, with the header repeated. Use the view buttons under the page: Page, Spread (facing pages) or All pages (grid, click a page to open it).

5. Style and trim

Clean White, Kraft, Farmhouse, Print-safe B/W, or Match My Cover (8 colors taken from your cover image with text contrast of at least 4.5:1). Trim sizes 6x9, 8x10, 8.5x11 and A4 with safe margins you can change.

Edit the text on the pages

Editing is on by default (the button under the pages says "Editing: on"; click it to lock the pages). Click any text on a page (titles, labels, table cells, notes) and type. Press Enter when done. Your edits are saved with the project, appear in the PDF, PNG ZIP and print, and are kept when you change other settings. Text inside charts and drawings cannot be edited this way. Reset text edits brings the original text back. If you change the data behind a text, that one edit is dropped because the original text changed.

6. Export

Exact PDF and PNG ZIP capture the pages exactly as shown. Exact Print sets the paper size and opens the print dialog. If an export fails you get a red message and a "Print instead" button. Save project stores everything in one .json file; the browser also autosaves.

7. Troubleshooting

  • A number is refused: read the red message under the field and enter a number that is 0 or more.
  • PDF fails: libraries load from the internet. Reload and retry, or use Exact Print.
  • Too many pages: untick pages in Book and Export.

8. Shortcuts

Alt+Left/Right pages, Alt+1 to Alt+7 tabs, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Y redo, Ctrl/Cmd+S save project, Alt+E Exact PDF, Alt+P Exact Print, F1 guide, Esc close.
