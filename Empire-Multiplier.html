📖 HOW TO USE (60-SECOND READ)
  1. Enter the seed series — title, trope, logline (paste from Trope Engine) and cast. This is the DNA every variant rebuilds.
  2. Choose scope (3–5 channels) and check ≥1 variant dimension (culture, language, register, demographic) — dimensions decide HOW channels differ.
  3. Be honest on capacity (real eps/week across everything). Hit Generate: each channel gets a unique name, tone, language lane, positioning angle and Same-DNA-vs-New comparison.
  4. Read the calendar honestly — launches stagger every 1–2 weeks; if the ⚠ warning fires, reduce scope or raise capacity before committing.
  5. Refine: 🔄 Regenerate re-rolls one channel only (naming + positioning stay unique plan-wide). Save per seed series; export the roadmap.
Pro tips: tones and languages are dealt uniquely per plan (no duplicates); positioning angles never repeat within a plan. The math assumes ~3 eps/week/channel over 10-week seasons + 3/week for the original — adjust capacity, not the formula.
