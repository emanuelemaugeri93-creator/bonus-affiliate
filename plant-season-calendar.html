How to use Planting and Season Calendar Studio

All dates and crop data here are TYPICAL estimates. Your local conditions differ. Check your local extension service advice and the seed packet for every crop before you plant.

Quick startFrost datesCropsHow dates are worked outThe pagesStyle and trimExportTroubleshootingShortcuts

1. Quick start

  1. Frost Dates: enter your last spring frost and first fall frost, or pick an example preset (examples only, not your location).
  2. Crops: tick the crops you grow. Click a crop to edit any number.
  3. Style: choose a look and a trim size. You can match your book cover colors by uploading the cover.
  4. Book Pages: choose which pages to include: title, how-to, year wheel, Gantt chart, month grid, week-by-week list, seed-starting table and a blank planner.
  5. Export: Exact PDF, PNG ZIP or Exact Print.

2. Frost dates

Use full dates. The growing season length is shown automatically. For the southern hemisphere enter the dates as they fall in your year (for example last frost 20 Sep and first frost 10 May of the next year) and set "Calendar year starts in" to July. The frost buffer moves tender crops later by the number of days you choose.

3. Crops

Built-in crops are grouped and searchable. For each crop you can change the indoor start week, the sowing or transplant week (weeks from last spring frost, negative = before), days to maturity, harvest window length in weeks, frost hardiness and succession planting (interval and number of plantings). Add your own crops at the bottom.

4. How dates are worked out

  • Planting date = last spring frost + weeks x 7 days. Harvest begins = planting (or transplant) date + days to maturity. Harvest window = the weeks you set (default 3).
  • Garlic is planted 4 weeks before the first fall frost and harvested the next summer.
  • Warnings: "May not mature before first fall frost" appears for frost-tender crops whose harvest would begin after the first fall frost. "No valid window" appears when the planting date falls on or after the first fall frost. Dates before the calendar year start are marked "previous year".
  • Succession planting repeats every N weeks only while the whole harvest window still ends before the first fall frost.

5. The pages

Year wheel (circular), Gantt chart (bars for indoors, growing and harvest), month grid, week-by-week to-do list, seed-starting schedule and a blank fill-in planner with your frost dates. Long lists continue on extra pages automatically.

Page layouts and extras

  • View layouts: Page shows one page, Spread shows two facing pages like an open book, All pages shows the whole book as a grid (click a page to open it).
  • Starter sets in Crops pick a group of crops in one click. Each group has Select all / none.
  • Mark a date draws a line on the wheel and Gantt chart (for example today).
  • Book Pages lets you reorder pages with Up / Down. Each crop also has a notes field that appears in the seed table and CSV.
  • Calendar file (.ics) and Schedule (CSV) are in Export.

6. Style and trim

Cozy Garden, Kraft, Clean White, Print-safe B/W (no color needed) and Match My Cover. Trim sizes: 6x9, 8x10, 8.5x11 and A4, with adjustable margins. Text is always dark or light enough to read (contrast of at least 4.5:1).

Edit the text on the pages

Editing is on by default (the button under the pages says "Editing: on"; click it to lock the pages). Click any text on a page (titles, labels, table cells, notes) and type. Press Enter when done. Your edits are saved with the project, appear in the PDF, PNG ZIP and print, and are kept when you change other settings. Text inside charts and drawings cannot be edited this way. Reset text edits brings the original text back. If you change the data behind a text, that one edit is dropped because the original text changed.

7. Export

  • Exact PDF and PNG ZIP capture each page exactly as shown. Exact Print sets the paper size and opens the print dialog (choose Save as PDF, margins None).
  • If an export fails you get a red message and a "Print instead" button.
  • Save project keeps everything in one .json file; the browser also autosaves.

8. Troubleshooting

  • "May not mature": choose a faster variety, start indoors earlier, or reduce the frost buffer.
  • No window: the first fall frost is too early for that crop, or the season is too short.
  • PDF fails: libraries load from the internet. Reload and try again, or use Exact Print.

9. Shortcuts

Alt+Left/Right pages, Alt+1 to Alt+5 panels, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Y redo, Ctrl/Cmd+S save project, Alt+E Exact PDF, Alt+P Exact Print, F1 this guide, Esc close.
