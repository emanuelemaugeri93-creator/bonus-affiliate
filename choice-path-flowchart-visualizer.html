GAMEBOOK MAPPER MANUAL — built for Gamebooks Empire:
  1. Sections are nodes. Hit + ADD NODE, click empty canvas, type the section (e.g. “Section 1: the bell rings at noon”). START is your Section 1. Drag beats anywhere.
  2. Paths draw themselves. Drag from a node's + handle onto another node to link them — or drag to empty space to auto-create a connected node. (Clicking the handle also works: then click a target.) Click any path to name it: Turn to 3, quick-picks, or re-point it elsewhere.
  3. Endings come in three flavors — BEST (all key clues), PARTIAL (thin proof), COLD CASE (unsolved, with a tip). Pick the kind when you add the node; the corner dot shows it.
  4. Portals jump maps. Open a second tab for Book 2 or a side branch, drop a violet PORTAL node, point it at the other map, hit » to travel. Portals never need outgoing paths.
  5. Validate like the Testing Pack. VALIDATE STRUCTURE runs the same spirit of checks: every section reachable (no broken “turn to” links), every choice with 2+ real options, every portal aimed somewhere. Red outlines mark the crime scenes — click the report to dismiss.
  6. Templates teach the shape. Load MAPLE HOLLOW (recipe-box mystery), MIDNIGHT LIBRARY, HAUNTED HARBOR or SUNKEN GALLEON — five ready maps total — then reshape everything. MINIMAL is the smallest valid map.
  7. Navigate: pinch with two fingers (trackpad or touchscreen) to zoom the whole map, drag the background — or hold SPACE and drag anywhere, nodes included — to move the entire map, FIT frames everything.
  8. Move many at once: hold SHIFT and drag a box around nodes to select them (white outline), then drag any of them to move the whole group — DEL deletes the selection, ESC or empty click clears it.
  9. Finish & ship. AUTO-LAYOUT columns your story by depth, FIT frames it, SNAP aligns to grid, ↶ ↷ undo anything, EXPORT PNG/SVG saves the active map for your records.
RULES OF THE BOARD: START can't be deleted · endings & portals take no outgoing paths · max 4 paths per node · double-click edits · × or right-click deletes · everything autosaves.
