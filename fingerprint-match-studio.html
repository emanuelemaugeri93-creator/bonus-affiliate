How to use Fingerprint Match Studio

1

Set up the book

Choose the number of cases and the difficulty. Ramping goes from Easy to Expert. Each case has 6 to 12 suspects with full prints and 1 to 3 partial prints from the scene.

2

Fill in My Case

Type your own case titles, victims, settings and suspect names. They go first, and the built-in banks fill the rest.

3

Match your cover

Upload the book cover and the From my cover preset takes its colours. Lock or change any swatch.

4

Trim for KDP

Pick the trim and bleed. The blue box is the safe area, and the red line is the inside margin, which grows with the page count. Guides never print.

5

Check and export

Answer view outlines each crop on the matching print and numbers the ridge features. Export one PDF with page numbers, or use the Print or save as PDF button at the very top.

6

Shortcuts

G generate, A answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
