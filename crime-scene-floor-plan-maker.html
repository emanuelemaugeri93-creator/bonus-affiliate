How to use

Make a top-down map of a crime scene for a mystery book page, a case file or a puzzle: rooms, furniture, the body, numbered evidence markers, footprints, suspects and a legend.

Quick start
  1. Pick a setting (manor, night train, ski lodge…). A floor plan appears.
  2. Press New floor plan until you like the layout. Every press draws a different building and a new crime.
  3. Type the case title, choose a style and a page size, then export the page.
Editing the map
  • Click anything to select it; drag to move it. The right panel lets you rename, rotate, resize, duplicate or delete it.
  • Double-click a room to rename it. Select a room and press Make this the crime room to move the body and the evidence there.
  • Add to the map: body outline, evidence marker, footprints, broken glass, blood drops, suspect, and furniture. New items land in the selected room.
  • Evidence legend: write what each numbered marker is. New descriptions suggests a fresh set.
  • Suspects: name them in the right panel. With "who was where?" on, your map becomes a deduction puzzle.
  • Handwritten note: a red note with an arrow ("Window forced open?"); double-click it to change the words, rotate it to point the arrow.
  • Rooms: Fewer / Normal / More changes how many rooms the next plan has. Title idea writes a new case title.
  • Undo / Redo (Ctrl or Cmd + Z, Shift for redo) for every change. Keys: Delete removes the selected item, R rotates it, arrows nudge it.
  • Detective questions: tick it to print up to 5 questions under the map, worked out from your plan (where the body was, who was where, which rooms connect). Answer key exports the same page with the answers filled in.
  • My plans: save as many plans as you like and reopen them later; Export makes a backup file, Import loads it on another computer.
Styles and pages
  • 9 styles: Blueprint, Pencil, Ink noir, Case file, Chalkboard, Neon, Old map, Police report, Print B&W.
  • Print B&W is pure black on white: best for KDP interiors (cheaper ink, crisp lines).
  • Square suits inserts and social posts; 8.5×11 and 6×9 are full book pages with safe margins.
Exports
  • Page as PNG: 300 DPI image, ready for an image page in your book software.
  • Page as PDF: exact page size, ready to merge into your interior.
  • Plan only: transparent background, to place on covers or other designs.
  • Clean version: the same rooms without body, markers or suspects, for a "before" page or a spot-the-difference.
  • Mockups: your page pinned on an evidence board and inside a case folder, for listings and social posts.

Page sizes

  • Square · 8 × 8 in (2400 px): inserts, social posts, Etsy.
  • 8.5 × 11 · large puzzle books, 0.5 in margins.
  • 6 × 9 · travel size, 0.5 in margins.
  • Wide · 11 × 8.5 landscape: two-page spreads and posters.
