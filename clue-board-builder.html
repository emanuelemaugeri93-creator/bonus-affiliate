How to use Clue Board Builder

Plan a paranormal mystery on a detective corkboard. The checker tells you whether the case really holds together.

Step by step

  1. Generate a case to start.Open the Generate tab on the right, choose a case type (murder, curse, heist...), a twist and how many clues you want, then press Generate Case. You can also type a supernatural rule of your world (for example "vampires cannot cross running water") and it will be woven into the clues.
  2. Read the board from left to right.The pink Incident card is where the case begins. The clues, suspects and locations follow. The gold Reveal is the ending. Orange cards at the bottom are Herrings (false leads) and the green Debunk cards disprove them.
  3. Follow the red strings.A string with an arrowhead means "this card points to that card". It shows the path your detective can follow.
  4. Move and edit the cards.Drag a card by its top strip (not the round pin). Click inside a card to change the text. Drag the little corner to resize it. With a card selected, the arrow keys nudge it (hold Shift to move further).
  5. Tie or cut strings.Click the round pin of one card, then the pin of another: a new string appears. To remove a string, click it on the bare cork, or use the Cut buttons in the Card tab. On a keyboard: select a card and press L, then select another card and press L again.
  6. Watch the Case checker.The badge in the top bar turns green when the mystery works and red when it does not. Open the Checker tab for plain-English reasons, such as "the culprit is too close to the start" or "a clue leads nowhere". Cards that cause a problem get a red outline.
  7. Turn the board into chapters.The Outline tab converts your clue chain into chapter beats, each with the cost to your lead. Download it as .md or copy it into your writing plan or the Royalties Bot.
  8. Save your work.The board saves itself in this browser. Use Save to keep a .json file you can reopen later with Load, and PNG to export a picture of the board with your name on it.

Good to know

  • Made a mistake? Undo and Redo remember your last 100 steps.
  • Press Auto-layout to tidy the cards back into neat lanes after lots of dragging.
  • Start from a Blank board and add your own cards with + Add card if you already have a story in mind.
  • Use Fit to see the whole board, and + / - to zoom. On a phone, slide the board with your finger.
  • To mark who did it, select a Suspect card and tick This suspect is the culprit in the Card tab.

If something looks wrong

  • The checker says "no culprit marked"? Select the right Suspect card and tick the culprit box in the Card tab.
  • A card is hard to grab? Grab its top strip, not the pin in the middle (the pin starts a string).
  • Something broke after loading a file? A red message explains what is wrong with the file. Your current board stays untouched.
