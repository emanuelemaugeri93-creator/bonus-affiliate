Guide: how this tool works

What you are making

A memory-match game: face-down cards, the player flips two at a time and tries to find pairs. You decide what each pair is (a picture and its name, a term and its meaning, an object and its era). You build it here, test it, and download one .html file to sell, share or give away as a lead magnet. You can also print the cards on paper.

Quick start (5 steps)

  1. Pairs tab. A starter pack is already loaded. Change the pairs, or pick another pack (6 included, 12 pairs each), or press + Add pair, or paste a list in Bulk paste. Each pair has side A, side B and an optional fact.
  2. Look tab. Pick the card back pattern, card style, board colours and flip style. Watch the Live preview on the right.
  3. Modes tab. Choose which game modes players get, which board sizes, the scoring, your title, texts, button link and logo.
  4. Test tab. Read the checks (they tell you what to fix), run the board check, then press Play-test.
  5. Export tab. Download game gives you the .html file. Print card sheet makes cut-out cards. Download project saves your work to edit later.

Pairs: the rules

  • You need 3 to 18 pairs. The biggest board (6x6) uses 18. A board is only offered when you have enough pairs for it.
  • No two pairs may look the same (a duplicate side makes the right match unclear). The checks flag it and the Fix it button jumps to the problem.
  • Pictures are cropped to a centred square and shrunk to 400 px automatically. Short text works best: long text is shrunk to fit the card.
  • The fact (up to 140 characters) is shown when the pair is matched in Learn mode and in the results recap.
  • Verify facts before selling. The starter packs use short, well-known facts, but you are responsible for every fact in your game.

Bulk paste format

Amphora,Ancient Greece,"Used to carry wine, oil and grain"
"Quill, goose",Medieval scribe
Telescope,1608

One pair per line: A, B and an optional fact. Put quotes around text that contains a comma. Empty lines are ignored.

The game modes

  • Classic: match everything. Timed: beat a countdown. Move limit: finish within a number of moves.
  • Memory Master: levels from 2x3 up to 6x6, score carries over. Two players: take turns on one device, a match earns another turn.
  • Learn: shows the fact each time you match a pair. Daily challenge: the same board for everyone each day.
  • Players can use hints (a quick peek at one pair), earn up to 3 stars for few moves, and see a best score saved on their own device.

Tips

  • 12 pairs gives a good range of boards. 6 pairs is enough for a small, quick game.
  • Keep each side short and clear. Use two different pictures of the same thing for Image to Image pairs.
  • Everything saves automatically in this browser. Ctrl or Cmd + Z undoes (50 steps).
  • Only upload pictures you made or are allowed to sell.
