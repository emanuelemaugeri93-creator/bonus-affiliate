    How to use the Sticker Sheet Studio

    1

    Add stickers

    Drop transparent PNG files, or press Add Starter Set. For each sticker set the quantity, the target width and whether to include it.

    2

    Set the sticker border

    In Cut & Marks choose the border width. The die-cut line follows the art at an even distance on every side.

    3

    Pick the sheet and pack it

    Choose a sheet size and spacing. Press Shuffle layout for another arrangement. Check the warning list: every sticker must stay inside the printable box.

    4

    Cricut or Silhouette

    Print page 1 of each sheet at 100% size. The page after it shows the cut lines in magenta for preview only. Upload the SVG cut file if you want your machine to cut the exact contours. The printable box and the mark positions are only a starting point: verify them in your own Design Space or Silhouette Studio version before selling.

    5

    Digital stickers

    Switch the mode to Digital. Export transparent PNGs for GoodNotes and Notability, with an optional white border and soft shadow.

    6

    Rights and testing

    Convert only artwork you own or have the right to use. Print and cut one test sheet before you sell in bulk.

    7

    Shortcuts

    G shuffle layout, 1 2 3 Art / Cut lines / Both, ← → sheets, + - zoom, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+P print, H help.
