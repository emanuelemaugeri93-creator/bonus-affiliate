How to use Analog Reset

1

Generate your week pages

Set the page count (1-20), energy level, week angle and starting day. Press Generate. Every sheet prints its seed in the footer, so any page can be reproduced exactly.

2

Flip between views

Week Page shows the full seven-day sheet. Day Cards shows big single-day cards, two per page — handy for fridge doors and classrooms.

3

Style it live

Pick a preset (try Print-safe B/W for cheap interiors), tune colors, borders and paper size. Every control updates the preview instantly. All icons are drawn vectors.

4

Preview and export

Download Exact PDF renders week pages first, then day cards. Exact Print gives one page per sheet. Copy listing text drafts your Etsy description in one click.

5

Tips

Arrow keys flip sheets, H opens this guide. The weekend wildcard adds a bonus box for Saturdays that spill over. Tick circles are meant to be circled with a real pen.
