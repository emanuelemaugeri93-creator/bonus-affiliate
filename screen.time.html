How we calculated this

dailyHours = the sum of your category sliders. With weekends enabled: (5 × weekday + 2 × weekend) ÷ 7.

yearHours = dailyHours × 365. yearDays = yearHours ÷ 24.

share of waking life = dailyHours ÷ wakingHours. yearsLeft = max(0, lifeExpectancy − age).

lifetimeYears = dailyHours ÷ 24 × yearsLeft. reclaimedDaily = dailyHours × reclaimShare, and reclaimedYear = reclaimedDaily × 365.

Calendar averages use only the days you have saved: average = total hours ÷ days logged. Your calendar stays in this browser unless you clear its data.
