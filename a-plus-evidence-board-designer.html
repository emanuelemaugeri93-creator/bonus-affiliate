How to use

A+ Content is the image section under "From the Publisher" on your Amazon book page. This tool designs the whole section for you: every image at the exact size KDP asks for, plus the headline, body text and image keywords to paste into the KDP A+ editor.

Quick start
  1. Book: pick the type of book (mystery puzzle, puzzle, activity or coloring), type your title and details, upload the cover and drop your interior PDF (or page images). A sample book is loaded so you can try first.
  2. Modules: tick the modules you want and put them in order. Style: pick one of 12 looks and an accent colour.
  3. Click a module on the page to change its layout (◀ ▶), edit any text right on the image, then press All modules (ZIP).
  4. In KDP: Marketing → A+ Content → create content, add the same modules in the same order, upload each image and paste the texts from the right panel.
The modules
  • Brand banner (970 × 600), Headline strip (970 × 300), Difficulty ladder (970 × 300), Perfect gift for (970 × 300).
  • Look inside (4 × 220 × 220), Three highlights (3 × 300 × 300), How to solve (3 × 300 × 300), Zoom on a page (300 × 300).
  • Series comparison (150 × 300 per volume) and Author logo (600 × 180).
Editing
  • Click any text on an image: rewrite it, change size, colour, font or capitals, drag it, hide it or reset it.
  • Rewrite all texts writes fresh headlines and lines for every module from your book details. Undo / Redo (Ctrl or Cmd + Z) for every change.
  • The right panel holds the KDP text fields of the selected module: copy each one into the A+ editor. Image keywords (alt text) are required by KDP for every image.
A+ checks
  • The checks warn you about words Amazon rejects in A+ Content (prices, "free", "sale", "bestseller", customer reviews, time-limited offers), missing image keywords and text that is too small to read on a phone.
Exports and sets
  • All modules (ZIP): every image named in page order, plus a text file with all KDP texts. Page preview: a picture of the finished A+ section.
  • My A+ sets: save a set (with its images) and reopen it later; Export makes a backup file.

Content rules to remember
  • No prices, discounts, "free", promotions or time-limited language ("new", "limited time", "sale").
  • No customer reviews, star ratings or "bestseller" claims.
  • No links, emails or contact details.
  • Every image needs image keywords (alt text).
