How to use Surveillance Camera Grid Studio

1

Set up the book

Choose how many puzzles you want, the difficulty and the grid size. Ramping goes from Easy to Hard. Every floor plan has exactly one solution.

2

Fill in My Case

Add your book title, series, author, a dedication and your own venue names.

3

Match your cover

Upload the book cover and the From my cover preset takes its colors. Lock or change any swatch.

4

Trim for KDP

Pick the trim and bleed. The blue box is the safe area and the red line is the inside margin. Guides never print.

5

Check and export

Answer view shows the cameras, the watched squares and the first deductions. Export one PDF with page numbers, or use the Print or save as PDF button at the very top.

6

Shortcuts

G generate, A answer view, ← → pages, + - zoom, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+P print, H help.
