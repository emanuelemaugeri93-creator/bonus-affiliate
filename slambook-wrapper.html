How to use — 4 steps
  1. Pick a style — 4 packages match SlamBook Forge exactly (Pastel / Chibi / Storybook / Crayon).
  2. Fill details — title, theme, child's name & compiled by. Add other themes you own for the back matter.
  3. Choose paper & pages — US Letter / Square / A4, toggle which pages to include.
  4. Export — PDF for print, PNG/JPG for preview. Insert your 12 Forge pages between the wrapper pages.
