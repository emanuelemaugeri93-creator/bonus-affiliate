How to use Homestead Log Book Studio

Design complete log book interiors and export a KDP-ready PDF. The logs are for record-keeping only: no instructions or advice are printed on any page.

Quick startLogs and layoutsColumnsKDP marginsBook pagesEdit textExportTroubleshootingShortcuts

1. Quick start

  1. Log Types: tick the logs you want (pantry, harvest, seed starting, livestock, maintenance, outage, water, expense, weather). Set the pages for each and choose a layout.
  2. Columns: click a log name and choose its columns from the library.
  3. Style: pick a paper style (or match your cover colors), the row height and the seed code.
  4. Book: fill in My Brand, choose the trim size and the front pages you want.
  5. Export: Exact PDF for KDP, PNG ZIP, or Exact Print.

2. Logs and layouts

Each log can use one of four layouts: Table (columns and rows), Ruled journal (lines), Dot grid, or Calendar month. The row height defaults to 0.3 in. You get a warning below 0.25 in because writing gets cramped. Each log has 2 to 60 pages.

3. Columns

The library has date, item, quantity, unit, location, expires, source, cost, weight, notes, check box, initials, temperature (free fill) and rating. Widths are fitted automatically. If too many columns are chosen for the page, the last ones are left out and a warning tells you which, so every column stays readable. You can rename the item column (for example Crop, Animal, Task).

4. KDP margins

The inside (gutter) margin follows your total page count: 24 to 150 pages 0.375 in, 151 to 300 pages 0.5 in, 301 to 500 pages 0.625 in, 501 to 700 pages 0.75 in, 701 to 828 pages 0.875 in. Margins are mirrored: odd pages (right) have the gutter on the left, even pages on the right. Turn on bleed to add 0.125 in. Always verify against current KDP guidelines.

5. Book pages

Title page, copyright page, "How to use this log book" page, abbreviations table, an automatic index with page ranges, section divider pages with a tab marker, your logs in your chosen order, and notes pages. My Brand fields (author or shop, series, dedication, website) fill the front pages.

Make it your own

  • Rename everything: in Columns you can rename the log, the item column, every column header and the header fields (Name, Year, Season...).
  • Add and reorder: add your own logs (or duplicate one), add Custom 1-3 columns and a numbered # column, and move columns Up / Down.
  • Total row: turn on a Total row at the bottom of each table page (handy for costs and weights).
  • Lines and shading: choose thin, normal or bold lines and switch row shading on or off in Style.
  • Even page count: one tick adds a notes page when the total would be odd.

6. Edit text on the pages

The bar above the pages says "Editing: on". Click it to lock the pages (so you cannot change them by accident) and click again to edit.

Editing is on by default: click any text on a page (titles, column headers, labels, notes) and type. Press Enter when done. Edits to a log's header or column titles apply to every page of that log. You can also type into the empty cells and lines to pre-print entries (for example a list of animals). Choose whether cell text goes on every page of the log or only on this page. Edits are saved with the project and appear in the PDF. Reset text edits restores the original text.

7. Export

Exact PDF captures each page at your chosen quality. Large books take time: you see progress and can cancel. Use the page range to export part of a book. PNG ZIP gives one image per page. Exact Print opens the print dialog with the paper size set. If an export fails you get a red message and a "Print instead" button.

8. Troubleshooting

  • Columns left out: choose fewer columns or a bigger trim size.
  • Row height warning: use 0.25 in or more.
  • Export is slow: use Draft quality or export a page range.
  • PDF fails: libraries load from the internet. Reload and retry, or use Exact Print.

9. Shortcuts

Alt+Left/Right pages, Alt+1 to Alt+5 panels, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Y redo, Ctrl/Cmd+S save project, Alt+E Exact PDF, Alt+P Exact Print, F1 this guide, Esc close.
