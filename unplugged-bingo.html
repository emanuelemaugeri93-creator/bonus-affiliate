How to use Unplugged Bingo

1

Generate your cards

Set the card count (1-12), week theme and style. Press Generate. Every card is balanced across activity types and carries its seed in the footer, so any card can be reproduced exactly.

2

Read a card

25 squares (or 24 plus a FREE star when enabled). Do an activity, daub the square. Five in a row — across, down or diagonal — wins the round.

3

Style it live

Pick a preset (try Print-safe B/W for cheap interiors), set your shop branding, tune colors and borders. Every control updates the preview instantly.

4

Preview and export

Download Exact PDF renders cards first, then the calling sheet. Card PNG, Calling CSV and Copy calling list cover the rest. Exact Print gives one page per sheet.

5

Tips

Arrow keys flip sheets, H opens this guide. Cut the calling sheet into strips and draw from a bowl. The seed in the footer reproduces that exact card.
