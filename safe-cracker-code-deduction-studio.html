How to use Safe Cracker Studio

1

Set up the book

Choose how many puzzles you want and the difficulty. Ramping goes from Easy to Expert. Every puzzle has one code that fits all its clues.

2

Fill in My Case

Add your book title, series, author, a dedication and your own case names.

3

Match your cover

Upload the book cover and the From my cover preset takes its colors. Lock or change any swatch.

4

Trim for KDP

Pick the trim and bleed. The blue box is the safe area and the red line is the inside margin. Guides never print.

5

Check and export

Answer view shows the code on the vault dial and the deduction steps. Export one PDF with page numbers, or use the Print or save as PDF button at the very top.

6

Shortcuts

G generate, A answer view, ← → pages, + - zoom, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+P print, H help.
