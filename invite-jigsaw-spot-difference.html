How to use Invite Jigsaw and Spot the Difference

1

Start from your invitation

Upload the invitation artwork you already made. With no upload the studio uses a built-in party scene, so you can try everything right away.

2

Real jigsaw cuts

Every inside edge is one smooth knob curve, shared exactly with its neighbour, so cut pieces fit. Choose 12 to 48 pieces or large kids tabs. Export the cut pattern as an SVG for cutting machines.

3

Spot the differences

The picture is edited in 5, 7, 10 or 12 places: colour, brightness, missing objects, flipped or resized patches, swapped patches and added shapes. The app checks every difference is visible and that the count is exact.

4

Print and sell

Pick 5 x 7, square, Letter or A4 with crop marks. Export a 300 DPI PDF or PNG ZIP. Answer pages are added at the end.

5

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
