📖 HOW TO USE THIS TOOL (60-SECOND READ)
  1. Paste ONE episode beat — a single sentence from your Trope Engine series (or your own), e.g. She slaps him across the face at the gala.
  2. Set the cast ONCE — paste the full cast list in Full Cast Reference (it auto-saves); per episode, just list who's in the scene.
  3. Lock the style — use the SAME style value as Face-Lock Forge so faces and footage match. 9:16 vertical is always locked.
  4. Hit Segment — you get 4 ordered 15-second prompts: Establish → Develop → Escalate → Cliffhanger.
  5. Copy top-to-bottom into your video generator (Step 4 workflow), one clip at a time, in sequence.
  6. Save every episode — work to 30/30 on the progress bar, then Copy Full Season for all 120 prompts.
Pro tips: 🔀 Alternate Cut re-shoots the same beat with all-new coverage (great for A/B picks). Keep character name spelling IDENTICAL everywhere or continuity breaks. The Cliffhanger clip sells the NEXT episode — reroll until the Escalate clip feels biggest.
