How to use Language Puzzle Studio

1

Choose language, level and units

Pick Spanish, French, Italian or German, a CEFR level (A1, A2, B1) and the themes you want. Each theme becomes a unit: a vocabulary page followed by your chosen puzzles. Press Generate Deck.

2

Puzzles

Word search, crossword from translation clues, matching columns, scrambled sentences and fill-in-the-blank with a word bank. Difficulty changes grid size, word count and whether the word list is English or the target language.

3

Accents and pronunciation

Strict keeps accents in the grids; Beginner removes them in grids but keeps them in lists. Pronunciation hints are simple English-style respellings, not IPA, and are approximate.

4

Your own words

Paste "word = translation" lines (up to 60). Bad lines are listed in red. Your words get their own unit and are used in the word searches, crosswords and matching pages.

5

Book and export

Pick a KDP trim, bleed and check the guides. Book Builder adds title, copyright, how-to, glossary and answers. Export one PDF or a PNG ZIP. The seed in every footer rebuilds the same deck.

6

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
