How to use Puzzle Pin Studio

1. Add your book

Upload your cover (only a cover you own). The studio reads 8 main colors and builds pin colors with readable text. No cover? Pick one of 6 built-in palettes. Fill in the title, author, genre, call to action and the text that points to your page.

2. Choose puzzle types and generate

Maze, mini sudoku, word search, cipher line and odd one out. Every puzzle is checked by a solver and has exactly one answer. Press Generate pins (or G).

3. Edit a pin

Click any card. The drawer lets you change the puzzle type, the seed, the headline, the layout and the colors, and edit the title, description, alt text and board. Counters show the limits.

4. Export

Press Export ZIP + CSV (Ctrl+E). You get the PNG pins, the optional Solution pins, a CSV and a contact sheet. The CSV has: pin file name, title, description, alt text, destination link, publish date. Check your Pinterest or scheduler import template and map the columns yourself.

5. Good to know

  • The same seed always gives the same pin.
  • Nothing is uploaded: everything runs in your browser. Autosave keeps settings only, never the cover.
  • Shortcuts: G generate, arrow keys move between cards, Enter opens a card, Esc closes, Ctrl+E export.
  • Use only covers you own or have the right to use.
