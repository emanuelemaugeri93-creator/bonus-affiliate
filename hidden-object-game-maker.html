How to use this tool (click to hide / show)
  1. 1 Scene - upload as many pictures as you like (PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 15 MB each) or press Generate a new scene for an instant ready-made one (endless: every click is a different scene with the hidden objects already marked). Each picture is one level. Name each level and add a one-line intro.
  2. 2 Hotspots - pick Rectangle, Ellipse or Polygon and draw around each hidden object. Give it a name (and optionally a riddle, a hint, easy/hard, or tick Decoy). Zoom with the mouse wheel, pan with Space + drag. Fix any yellow warnings in the Checks list.
  3. 3 Rules & look - choose the mode (list, silhouette, riddle), timer, penalties, hints, scoring, one of 6 themes, and your texts, button link and logo.
  4. 4 Test & export - press Play-test, then Download game (.html). Upload that single file to your own site or sell it as it is. Download project saves your work to edit later.

Which picture? A detailed, cluttered, high-resolution scene (at least 1600 px wide) made with AI or your own art, with plenty of small distinct objects: a workshop, market, library, ship, castle. Avoid text, logos and copyrighted characters. 5-25 objects per scene works well.

Tips: your work autosaves in this browser. Ctrl+Z undoes. Arrow keys nudge a selected shape. Keep the final file under 8 MB (the tool warns you and offers smaller image qualities).

You are responsible for the images you upload: only use pictures you created or have the right to use and sell. Everything you build is saved automatically in this browser.
