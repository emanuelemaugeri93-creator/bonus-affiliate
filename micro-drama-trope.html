📖 HOW TO USE (60-SECOND READ)
  1. Pick a trope (12 proven micro-drama engines), then Emotional Register, POV and Cast Size (5–6).
  2. Hit Generate — you get a title, logline, named 5–6 person cast, three-act spine and all 30 episode beats as a production checklist.
  3. Every generation differs — new B-plot woven into episodes 4-9-14-19-24, alternate openers, wild-card cast members and fresh names. Same trope never repeats itself.
