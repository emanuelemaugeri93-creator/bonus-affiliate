How to use

Make a short video that flips through your book, with a hook, a caption on each page and an end card with your button. Use it as a TikTok, Reel or Short, on your Amazon author page or in ads.

Quick start
  1. Pages: drop your book's PDF (or page images). Pick which PDF pages to use. Sample pages are loaded so you can try it first.
  2. Motion: choose an animation, a background and a format.
  3. Words: write the hook, a caption per page and the end card, then press Download video.
Pages
  • PDFs are turned into pages right in your browser (nothing is uploaded). Use "PDF pages from / to" to skip blank or title pages.
  • Drag pages to change the order; ✕ removes a page. Set cover image adds the front cover (used by "Book opens", the thumbnail and the end card).
Animations
  • Page flip and Book opens: a 3D book turning its pages. Slow zoom: each page fills the screen with a gentle zoom. Fan out: pages spread like cards, then come forward one by one. Page stack: pages slide in and land on a pile. Magnifier: a lens glides over each page. Grid reveal: every page pops into a grid, then zooms in. Puzzle → solution: shows a page, then wipes to the next one (great for puzzle and answer pages).
Edit on the video
  • Click the hook, a caption, the end title, line, button or badge on the preview: rewrite it, change size, colour, font and capitals, drag it, hide it or reset it.
Sound
  • Mystery or soft background music and page-turn sounds are generated by the tool, so they are yours to use with no copyright claims. They are recorded into the video.
Exports and projects
  • Download video records the trailer in real time (MP4 in recent Chrome and Safari, otherwise WebM). Keep the tab in front while it records.
  • Look-inside storyboard: all pages in a grid with captions, perfect for listing images and Pinterest.
  • My trailers: save trailers with their pages and reopen them later; Export makes a backup file.
  • Export all 4 formats records the same trailer in 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9, one after the other.
  • Corner signature: your @handle or website in a corner of every frame. Story-style progress bar: segments at the top that fill as the pages go by.
  • Undo / Redo (Ctrl or Cmd + Z) for pages, captions, texts and look. Shortcuts: Space play/pause, S surprise me.
