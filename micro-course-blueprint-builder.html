Step 1 of 5 — Niche & Audience

What world is this course in?

Name the niche and the exact human being this course serves. Specific beats broad — "budget meal prep for night-shift nurses" outsells "cooking" every time.

Step 2 of 5 — Transformation

What changes for them?

Finish this sentence: "After this course, my student can…" — this single line becomes your course promise, your sales headline, and the spine of every module.

Tip: start with an action verb (plan, build, launch, cook, write…). Avoid "I want to" or "they will be able to" — just the outcome.
Step 3 of 5 — Depth & Format

How deep does it go?

Pick the course size that fits your audience's patience — and the format you'll actually finish recording.

Step 4 of 5 — Price Positioning

Where does it sit on the shelf?

Price is positioning, not just money. Pick the tier that matches how boldly you want to sell the transformation.
