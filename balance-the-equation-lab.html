How to use Balance the Equation Lab

1

Pick difficulty and types

Choose Easy to Expert (or Mixed), how many equations per page and which of the five worksheet types you want. Pick the reaction categories, then press Generate Workbook.

2

Every answer is checked

The 90 built-in reactions are balanced by an exact integer null-space solver that finds the smallest whole-number coefficients. Atoms are recounted on both sides before anything is printed.

3

Your own equations

Paste one equation per line without coefficients, for example Fe + O2 -> Fe2O3. Brackets like Ca(OH)2 work. Lines that cannot be balanced, or have several answers, are listed in red and skipped.

4

Make a book

Choose a KDP trim and bleed, check the guides, and use Book Builder for the title, copyright and how-to pages plus an answer key. Export one PDF or a PNG ZIP. The seed in every footer rebuilds the same workbook.

5

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
