How to use Budget Quest

1

Pick levels and types

Level 1 is pennies, nickels and dimes up to $1.00. Level 5 reaches $100 with change and long shopping lists. Choose which of the six worksheet types to include, then press Generate Workbook.

2

Exact answers

All money is stored in whole cents, so there are no rounding errors. The fewest-coins answers are found by dynamic programming and every change and savings answer is recomputed.

3

Your own shop

Paste items as "name = price" and name your shop. Add student names for the stories. Lines that cannot be read are listed in red.

4

Make a book

Choose a KDP trim and bleed. Book Builder adds the title, copyright and money chart pages, a Money Master Certificate with an XP tracker, and an answer key. Export one PDF or a PNG ZIP; the seed in every footer rebuilds the same workbook.

5

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
