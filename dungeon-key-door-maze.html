How to use this studio

1

Pick your options

Choose how many maps, the difficulty, the grid size, how many locked doors and the key style. Expert unlocks teleport pads and one-way doors.

2

Generate

Press Generate (or G). Every map is built, then solved by code. If a map cannot be solved it is thrown away and rebuilt, so every printed puzzle has a real answer.

3

Make it yours

My World swaps in your own room, boss, key and treasure names. Match My Cover reads your cover image and builds a matching palette. Style changes the page look.

4

Check the answers

Press A to flip between puzzle pages and answer pages. The grey box under the controls shows the minimum steps, the key order and how many shortest routes exist for the map you are looking at.

5

Set up for KDP

KDP Trim Studio sets the trim size and bleed. Book Builder adds the title page, copyright page, how-to page, section dividers and answer key, and keeps the page numbers correct.

6

Export

Exact PDF and PNG ZIP are made in your browser at 300 DPI. SVG gives you a vector copy of a map, or a ZIP of all maps with their answers. Exact Print opens a print-ready window.

7

Keyboard shortcuts

G generate. A puzzles/answers. Left and right arrows change page. Ctrl or Cmd + Z undo, Ctrl or Cmd + Y redo. H help. Esc closes this window.
