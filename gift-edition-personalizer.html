How to use

Turn your casebook into a personalised gift: a cover with their name, a dedication page, a detective ID card with their photo, a certificate, bookplates, a greeting card, a wanted poster and a one-page mystery where they are the detective. Sell it on Etsy or give it yourself.

Quick start
  1. Recipient: type their name, pick a title and one of 12 occasions. A dedication is written for you; edit it or press New message. Add a photo for the ID card and the wanted poster, then zoom and move it to frame the face.
  2. Their case: put friends or family in as suspects and choose who did it.
  3. Style: pick one of 10 styles (each has its own cover layout and decoration) and the book size. Then press Gift pack PDF and print.
The pieces
  • Cover and Dedication page and Their case are book pages at your trim size (add them to your book file, or print and slip them in).
  • Detective ID: front and back, credit-card size, with cut marks. Certificate: letter landscape. Bookplates: a sheet of 8 stickers. Greeting card: 5 × 7 folded card. Wanted poster: letter size, with their photo, what they are "wanted" for and a reward that matches the occasion.
  • Tick the box on each piece to put it in the gift pack.
Edit on the page
  • Click any text on the preview: rewrite it, change size, colour, font or capitals, drag it, hide it or reset it. Undo / Redo (Ctrl or Cmd + Z) for everything.
Selling on Etsy
  • Etsy listing images: 6 square images (2000 × 2000): a desk mockup, the whole kit, how personalisation works, close-ups, their own case with the wanted poster, and every style. Preview them, download one, or get all in a ZIP with the listing text.
  • Etsy listing text: a title, 13 tags (each under 20 characters), a description and the personalisation instructions to paste into Etsy.
  • Orders: paste your orders (Name | Occasion | Message | From), press Make all orders and get one PDF per customer in a ZIP.
Saving
  • My gifts keeps every gift (with its photo) on this device; Export makes a backup file.

Print sizes

  • Cover, dedication, case page: your trim size (6 × 9, 8.5 × 11 or 8 × 10 in), 300 DPI.
  • Detective ID: 3.375 × 2.125 in (credit card), front and back on one letter page with cut marks.
  • Certificate: 11 × 8.5 in landscape.
  • Bookplates: 8 labels of 3.5 × 2.25 in on a letter sheet (fits most full-sheet sticker paper).
  • Greeting card: 5 × 7 in folded (10 × 7 flat), front and inside.
  • Wanted poster: 8.5 × 11 in portrait.
  • Print at "actual size" / 100% for exact measurements.
