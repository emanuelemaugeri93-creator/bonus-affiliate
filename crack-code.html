How to Use Crack the Code Studio
Complete guide • Steps • Tips • Cipher Legend • FAQ

1️⃣ Choose Your Audience

Select the age group for your puzzle book:

  • 🧒 KIDS (5-8): Short 3-5 word sentences, large 32px symbols, simple vocabulary, bright coral theme.
  • 🧑 TWEENS (8-12): Medium sentences, 28px symbols, science & sports facts, navy blue theme.
  • 🧔 ADULTS: Complex phrases, idioms & quotes, 24px symbols, minimal dark theme.

2️⃣ Pick a Cipher Style

Each letter becomes a symbol. Choose from 4 unique cipher systems:

  • 🔢 NUMBERS: A=1, B=2, C=3... Z=26. Classic number substitution.
  • 🔷 SHAPES: Geometric symbols (circle, triangle, star, diamond...).
  • 🌟 ICONS: Thematic pictures (sun, moon, tree, anchor, bell...).
  • 👽 SYMBOLS: Alien-inspired invented glyphs, abstract and unique.

3️⃣ Select Category & Messages

Choose the theme for secret messages. Categories change based on audience:

  • Kids: Animal Facts, Silly Jokes, Nature Wonders, Food Facts, Friendship Messages.
  • Tweens: Science Facts, World Records, Sports Trivia, History Snippets.
  • Adults: Famous Quotes, Proverbs, Motivational Phrases, Tongue Twisters, Idioms.

Set Messages per page: 3-6. Fewer = larger symbols, more writing space.

4️⃣ Configure Decoder Key

The decoder key is the translation table. Control its appearance:

  • Position: Top of page (easier) or Bottom (more challenging).
  • Visibility - Full: All 26 letters shown. Best for beginners.
  • Visibility - Partial: Only letters used in the messages. Medium difficulty.
  • Visibility - Minimal: Random 50% of used letters hidden. Hard mode!

5️⃣ Generate & Print

Click "✨ Generate Worksheet" to create the puzzle page.

  • 🎲 Reshuffle: New random messages, same settings.
  • 👁️ Reveal One: Temporarily shows one decoded answer.
  • ➕ Add Page: Simulates multi-page worksheet.
  • 🖨️ Print: Opens print dialog. Use "Save as PDF" for KDP upload.

💡 PRO TIP: Hover over ANY label in the control panel for instant tooltips!

💡 Tips & Best Practices

  • 📝 Customize the Page Title and Subtitle to match your book theme.
  • ✅ Enable Writing Lines for a clean, classroom-ready look.
  • 🔑 Enable Answer Key for teacher/parent reference pages.
  • 📐 KDP requires 0.75" margins — this tool is pre-configured for print.
  • 🎨 Each audience has unique colors — the preview shows exactly what prints.

❓ Quick FAQ

  • Q: Can I use this for commercial KDP books?
    A: Yes! All content is original and royalty-free.
  • Q: How many unique puzzles can I make?
    A: With 270+ messages × 4 ciphers × 3 audiences × 3 key visibilities = thousands of combinations.
  • Q: Do SVG symbols print correctly?
    A: Yes, all symbols are inline SVG — they render perfectly in print and PDF.
