Amish Accuracy Defect Simulator — Detailed Guide
What this app is

This is the FINAL PASS companion for Amish Empire. While the megaprompt writes your novel, this tool teaches you to see the 7 kinds of accuracy slips it checks for — with side-by-side ❌ Flawed vs ✅ Corrected passages in authentic Amish-fiction voice. Use it to train your eye before you self-review a chapter.

The 7 defect categories
  1. Name Drift — surnames, nicknames, honorifics shifting.
  2. Timeline Contradiction — seasons, ages, build dates, church Sundays.
  3. Relationship Error — sister/cousin, bishop/deacon, lane geography.
  4. Ordnung Violation — phones, cars, dress, courtship, power — district-aware.
  5. Missing Romance Beat — slow-burn restrained gestures skipped.
  6. Repeated Scene — same reveal told twice as if new.
  7. Dangling Thread — promise with weight never paid off.
How the infinite engine works

Beyond the 44 hand-written curated pairs, the ♾️ Infinite toggle generates unlimited procedural variations from warm template banks (Amish first names, surnames, settlements like Hollow Creek / Bird-in-Hand, seasons, nouns, kinship terms). Each flawed/corrected pair is freshly assembled with consistent settlement context, so you never run out of drills. Toggle ♾️ Infinite: ON in the advanced bar — 35% of next examples will be generated.

How to use it (30-second drill)
  1. Pick a Defect Category or Surprise Me.
  2. Click Generate Example — read ❌ then ✅, then the Why This Matters note.
  3. Click 📋 Copy Self-Check Question and paste it into your chapter checklist.
  4. Use Show Another / Space to cycle, F to favorite, Search to find passages with a keyword.
Pro tip: Run the 7 self-check questions on every chapter before final pass — copy all 7 via 📋 Copy All Checks or export the full checklist. For Ordnung, set your settlement in the Settlement Ordnung Configurator so examples match YOUR district's rules.
