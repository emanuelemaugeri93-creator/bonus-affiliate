How to use Color-by-Note Piano Studio

1

Pick a level

Level 1 shows letter names. Level 2 shows notes on the treble staff. Level 3 mixes treble and bass clef. Level 4 adds sharps, flats, rests and note-value questions.

2

Pick a picture

Choose one of 40 pixel pictures or "Surprise me". Set how many colors to use. Every picture color gets its own note, chosen at random for each page.

3

Use your own image

Upload a png or jpg. It is turned into pixel art in your browser (up to 24 x 24). Move the sliders until it looks right, then press "Use my picture".

4

Print-safe B/W

That style replaces colors with distinct hatch patterns, so the legend still works on a black and white printer.

5

Make a book

Pick a KDP trim and bleed, then use Book Builder for the title, copyright and how-to pages with answers at the back. Export one PDF or a PNG ZIP. The seed in every footer rebuilds the same workbook.

6

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
