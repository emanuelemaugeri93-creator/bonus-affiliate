🎯 1. Audience Level

EARLY (4-6): 3-4 word sentences, large illustrated cards, simple vocabulary.

KIDS (6-9): 5-7 word sentences, moderate vocabulary, educational content.

TWEENS (9-12): 8-12 word sentences, complex grammar, challenging puzzles.

📂 2. Category & Mode

10 themed categories from Animals to Space & Science.

Number Mode: Write sequence numbers in boxes below each card.

Cut & Arrange: Dashed borders for physical cutting and pasting activity.

🎨 3. Card Style & Font

Plain: Clean bordered rectangles.

Rounded: Soft pill-shaped cards.

Illustrated: SVG icons above words (great for early readers).

Font sizes: Large (28px), Medium (20px), Small (14px).

🔀 4. Scramble Intensity

Gentle: First word stays in place as a starting hint.

Standard: All words shuffled, original capital letter preserved.

Full: All lowercase — no capital letter hint at all!

✨ 5. Generate & Print

3-6 sentences per page. Toggle answer key and word count hints.

Reshuffle: New word order for all sentences.

Reveal: Briefly shows correct numbering (2.5 seconds).

KDP-ready with 0.75" margins — just print or save as PDF.

💡 Pro Tips

• Use Illustrated style for preschool/K activity books.

• Combine Cut & Arrange with Gentle scramble for beginners.

• Full scramble + No word count = maximum difficulty.

• All sentences are educational, age-appropriate, and royalty-free.
