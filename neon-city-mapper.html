How to use Neon City Mapper

Build a fictional city map for your book in about a minute. No account, nothing to install.

Step by step

  1. Open the Controls panel.Tap the Controls button (top left) if the panel is closed. This is where you choose what kind of city you want.
  2. Pick a city type, size and tone.The type changes the shape (a coast, a river, mountains). The size is the number of districts: 6, 10 or 14. The tone only changes the mood of the names and descriptions.
  3. Choose how many factions and hidden entrances.Factions are the rival groups that own districts (2 to 6). Hidden entrances are the secret doorways your characters can use (3 to 12).
  4. Press Build City.Your map draws itself. Do not like it? Press Reroll for a brand new city. Like it? Copy the seed (the short code): typing the same seed with the same settings gives you the same city back.
  5. Explore the map.Hover a district to light it up. Click it (or a numbered diamond) and the Dossier opens on the right with its mood, danger level, a landmark, a street rumor and the hidden entrances inside it. Click a faction to see all its districts glow.
  6. Name the city and add your branding.Type your own city name, or press Name it for me. Fill in Author and Series: they are printed in the legend of every export.
  7. Choose a look.Neon Noir for screens and social media, Ink on Parchment for a fantasy feel, Blueprint for something technical, and Print B/W for the inside of a paperback (pure black, white and greys).
  8. Export what you need.PNG (3000 px wide, with paper or transparent background) for newsletters and merch, SVG for editing, Dossier .md for your notes, and Copy story bible to paste straight into the Story Bible step of the Urban Fantasy Royalties Bot.

Good to know

  • On a phone the map is wider than the screen: slide it with your finger, or use the + and - buttons at the top.
  • Use Labels and Grid overlay to simplify or add detail to the picture. They do not change the city, only how it is drawn.
  • Change a setting such as the size or the number of factions, then press Build City again: settings apply when you build.
  • Your author and series names are remembered on this computer, so you only type them once.

If something looks wrong

  • Nothing downloads? A red message at the top tells you what went wrong. Check that your browser is allowed to download files from this page, then try again.
  • The PNG uses a different font from the screen? That is normal: the picture is drawn without the web fonts. The map and the text are all there.
  • A label is missing on a small district? It was hidden to avoid overlapping. Its number badge is still on the map, and its name is in the Dossier.
