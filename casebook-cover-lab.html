How to use

Design a complete paperback cover for a mystery puzzle book (front, spine and back) sized exactly for Amazon KDP, then download the print-ready PDF.

Quick start
  1. Book tab: pick the trim size, type your page count and paper. The spine width is calculated for you.
  2. Words tab: title, subtitle, author, volume, cases badge and tagline. Look tab: style, palette, font and clue picture.
  3. Press Print-ready PDF and upload it to KDP as your paperback cover.
The inputs
  • Cover style: twelve mystery designs (Pixel Clue, Case File, Crime Tape, Noir Poster, Notebook, Evidence Board, Pulp Magazine, Fingerprint, Blueprint, Art Deco, Spotlight, Sealed Letter). New cover design shuffles style, palette and picture together.
  • Edit right on the cover: click any text on the front or back. A panel opens next to it: rewrite the text, make it bigger or smaller, change colour, font, alignment or capitals, hide it, or reset it. Drag the text to move it (arrow keys nudge it, Shift for bigger steps). Reset text edits puts every text back where the design had it.
  • Back tab: blurb, an "Inside" list, a sample puzzle and the white barcode box. New blurb writes a fresh back cover text.
  • Guides: red = trim line (anything past it is cut), blue = safe zone (keep text inside), green = spine. Guides never appear in exports.
KDP rules built in
  • Spine width = page count × paper thickness (white 0.002252 in, cream 0.0025 in, standard color 0.002252 in, premium color 0.002347 in).
  • 0.125 in bleed on every side; the cover PDF is (bleed + back + spine + front + bleed) wide.
  • Spine text only appears from 79 pages, as KDP asks.
  • The barcode box sits on the lower right of the back cover; leave it empty and KDP prints the barcode there.
Exports
  • Print-ready PDF: the full wrap at 300 DPI, exact KDP size.
  • eBook / front cover: the front only, 2560 px tall, for Kindle, Etsy and listings.
  • Mockups: five scenes (standing book, series stack, desk flat lay, front & back, eBook + print). Preview them in the Mockups view; All 5 mockups downloads every scene as a PNG.
Troubleshooting
  • KDP says the cover is the wrong size? Check that the page count here matches your interior PDF exactly.
  • Fonts look plain for a second? They load from Google Fonts: wait a moment, the preview redraws.

KDP paperback sizes

  • Puzzle books sell well in 8.5 × 11 (large, easy to write in) and 6 × 9 (travel size).
  • Large-print editions: 8.5 × 11 with big grids.
  • Square 8.5 × 8.5 works for gift and activity books.
  • Paperbacks need at least 24 pages; spine text needs at least 79.
