How to use Magic Rule Loophole Hunter

Write down the rules of your hidden world. The hunter asks the questions a sharp reader would ask, so you can fix plot holes before publishing.

Step by step

  1. Start with the example (optional).Press Load an example world to see eight finished rules and what the hunter does with them. You can erase it any time with Clear all.
  2. Add your rules in the Rules tab.One card per rule. Write a short statement (for example "A salt-priest can call a drowned person back within one tide"), choose the power type, say how common it is and who can use it.
  3. Fill in cost, limits and weakness.These three boxes are the heart of a good magic rule. Leave one empty and the hunter adds an extra question (and raises the danger score), so empty fields show you exactly what is missing. The orange tags on each card list the gaps.
  4. Go to the Hunt tab.You get a ranked list of questions: High danger first, then Medium, then Low. They include questions for each power, questions about how two of your rules clash, and questions about your whole world (police, money, secrecy, history, politics).
  5. Answer, then close the loophole.Write your answer in the box under each question. Not sure what to say? Tap one of the three fix chips (add a cost, add a limit, add an exception, make it rare, add a witness): it adds a starter sentence you can rewrite in your own words. Tick Resolved when you are happy.
  6. Watch the progress ring.The ring counts how many loopholes you have resolved. Closing a High-danger one gives you a little celebration.
  7. Tune the hunt.Questions per rule controls how deep it goes. The filters show only High questions, only open ones, or only one rule. Reroll order shuffles questions inside each danger group: the same seed always gives the same order.
  8. Build your rulebook.In the Rulebook tab you get one clean page per rule with your answers. Add your author and series name, then use Print / Save as PDF, Download Markdown, or Copy as story bible text to paste into the Urban Fantasy Royalties Bot.

Good to know

  • The danger score is explained on the Hunt tab: a heavy power (resurrection, time, mind control, immortality) with no cost scores highest.
  • Everything you type is saved automatically in this browser, so you can close the page and come back.
  • You do not have to answer every question: focus on the High ones, and answer the others only if they matter to your plot.
  • Use the up and down arrows on a rule card to reorder your rules; the order is the order of the rulebook pages.

If something looks wrong

  • Too many questions? Lower Questions per rule or filter by High.
  • The PDF looks different from the screen? The print version is a clean black-on-white page, one rule per page, on purpose.
  • Nothing to export? The exports need at least one rule. A red message tells you if something is missing.
