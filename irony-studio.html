3 Set Difficulty — Beginner offers guided questions. Advanced requires deep analysis of layered irony.

5 Analyze the Irony — Read the excerpt. Identify moments where characters don't know what readers already know. Circle the irony type that applies.

💡 Pro Tip: The most powerful dramatic irony comes from specific sensory details — a smell, sound, or texture that means one thing to the character and something entirely different to the reader.
