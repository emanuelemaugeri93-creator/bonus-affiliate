How to use Advent Puzzle Calendar

1

Pick your calendar

Choose 24 or 25 days, a difficulty ramp (Gentle, Steady, Challenge), an audience and which puzzle types to mix. Press Generate Deck. The seed in every footer rebuilds the same deck.

2

Every day gives one digit

Sudoku: the digit in the starred cell. Maze: corners on the route. Word search: words found backward. Cryptogram: different letters used. Number path: last digit of the highest number. Readers write it in the "Today's digit" box.

3

The finale

Digits from your chosen days (default 6, 12, 18, 24) open the treasure chest on the last page. Set the days and the prize message under Content Lock.

4

Make it a book

Pick a KDP trim, toggle bleed, check the dashed safe-area and gutter guides. Book Builder adds title, copyright and how-to pages and appends the answers. Export one PDF or a PNG ZIP.

5

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help. Your work autosaves in this browser.
