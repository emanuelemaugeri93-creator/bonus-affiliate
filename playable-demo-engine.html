READER'S GUIDE — from AI text to playable teaser in minutes:
  1. Build section by section. Each page = one gamebook section. Paste your AI passage into STORY TEXT — aim for 120–220 words (the counter under each ID watches this, just like the product rules).
  2. Link with choices. Every choice needs text plus a target: another Page ID or ★ THE END. Write them the classic way — “Turn to 3” style works, plain language works too.
  3. Score your endings. Mark ending pages and pick the kind that matches the clue score: Best (all key clues), Partial (thin proof), Cold case (unsolved + Detective's Tip). This is what makes readers replay.
  4. Set the start. One radio Set as Starting Page — that's your Section 1.
  5. Fill BOOK DATA. Title + author appear on the cover screen and inside the exported file.
  6. Clear PRE-FLIGHT. Red = broken (click any error to jump to that page). Yellow = warning, e.g. unreachable pages. Green means play.
  7. Play it like a kid. Cover → OPEN THE BOOK → click signposts (or keys 1–4). Dots track your path. Endings you find get a ★ NEW badge and stay counted on the cover.
  8. Ship it. EXPORT STANDALONE HTML builds the shareable teaser (email, A+ Content preview, screen-record). EXPORT JSON backs the project up.
  9. Play like a game. Story types itself out (click to skip, TYPE toggles it). Dots are clickable — jump back to any step. 📓 opens your detective notebook, 🔊 toggles retro sounds. Broken link in pre-flight? Hit ＋ CREATE and the missing page builds itself.
