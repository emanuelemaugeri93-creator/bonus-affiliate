How to Use This Tool

Quick Start

1. Paste your Secrets Ledger text into Import Secrets on the left (or click Load Sample Ledger to try it instantly).
2. Click Parse Secrets - you'll see how many secrets were found.
3. Pick a stage stamp from the toolbar above the grid, then click cells in each secret's row to place that stage at that book.
4. Check the Pacing Audit panel at the bottom for warnings about bunching, gaps, and reveals landing too close together.

The 4 Stages
  • 🔒 Hidden - not yet introduced or hinted.
  • 👁️ Suspected - a character or reader begins to suspect something.
  • 🔓 Partially Revealed - part of the truth surfaces, full scope still unknown.
  • ✅ Confirmed - fully revealed and resolved.
Scheduling

Select a stage from the stamp toolbar, then click any cell in a secret's row to place that stage marker at that book. Click a cell that already has that exact stage to remove it. Consecutive markers on a row are connected by a line showing the secret's reveal path across the saga.

Pacing Audit & Advanced Tools

This is the tool's real value: it automatically checks for reveals bunching up in one book, books with no movement at all, major reveals landing too close together (with extra strictness for High-severity secrets), and threads still unresolved by your final book - which you can mark as an intentional "seed for the next saga."

  • Pacing Health Score (top right of the summary strip) gives you one number for how well-spaced your whole schedule is.
  • Density Heatmap above the grid shows, at a glance, which books are quiet and which are overloaded.
  • 🧠 Suggest Rebalance analyzes any bunching and proposes a specific secret to move, and where.
  • Show only flagged rows filters the grid down to just the secrets currently involved in a warning.
Saving & Exporting

Everything autosaves to this browser. Use 💾 Save as New Saga to name and store a schedule, and the dropdown next to it to switch between saved sagas. 📋 Copy Schedule gives you a clean book-by-book text breakdown to paste back into your ledger or prompt library; 🖨️ Print gives you a wall-reference printout.

Why This Tool Exists

Your Secrets Ledger is rich. Your pacing is invisible.

The companion product generates deeply interconnected secrets, each staged across the 9-book arc — a piece revealed here, the full truth confirmed three books later. But as plain text, there's no way to see whether your reveals are bunching up in Book 4 while Book 7 sits empty, or whether two major confirmations are landing so close together they cancel each other's impact.

This tool turns that invisible pacing problem into something you can literally look at — a 9-book grid where every secret's reveal path is a visible line, and a live audit tells you exactly where the pacing is working and where it isn't.

1 Paste or load your Secrets Ledger — it parses into rows automatically.
2 Pick a stage and click cells across the grid to schedule each secret's reveal path.
3 Watch the Pacing Health Score and Audit panel tell you where to adjust.
