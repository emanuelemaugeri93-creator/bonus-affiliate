How to use Party Match Cards Studio

1

Pick the party

Choose the occasion and the audience. They pick the icon theme and the deck size: 7, 13, 21, 31 or 57 cards. Every card shares exactly one symbol with every other card.

2

Shape, size and symbols

Circle, rounded square or hexagon, 2.5, 3 or 3.5 inches. Use the 57 built-in icons or upload your own small png or svg images.

3

Match your invitation

Upload the invitation and the card colours, borders and backs follow it. You can also put the image on the card backs and on the box label.

4

Check two cards

Switch to Check, pick any two cards and the shared symbol pulses. Verify whole deck tests every pair.

5

Print

Double-sided sheets have backs mirrored to line up. The PDF holds the fronts, backs, a rules page with four games and a box label. Keep the footer seed so you can rebuild the deck.

6

Shortcuts

G generate, V cards/check, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
