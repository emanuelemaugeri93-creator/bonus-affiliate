How to use Rotation Rings

1

Generate your deck

Set the puzzle count (1-40) and difficulty - it chooses rings, spokes, digits and whether the target shows. Override anything manually, then press Generate. Every puzzle is brute-force checked for exactly one solution.

2

Style it live

Pick a preset, medallion, decoration and mandala tint. Every control updates the preview instantly. Ring A never moves; turn the others until every spoke matches.

3

Test-turn the rings

Click any ring on a puzzle page to rotate it one step with a smooth animation. This is only your scratch pad - the export always uses the pristine puzzle.

4

Preview and export

Toggle Puzzle / Answer View, browse layouts and zoom, reshuffle single sheets. Download Exact PDF renders puzzles first, then the key; Exact Print gives one page per sheet. Use Print-safe B/W for KDP interiors.

5

Tips

Write each ring's clockwise steps in the boxes under the board. If the spoke sum is hidden, it goes in the blank instead. The seed in the footer reproduces that exact puzzle.
