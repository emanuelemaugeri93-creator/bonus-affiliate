How to use Puzzle Invitation Studio

1

Fill in the party

Choose the occasion and audience, then type the details. For each detail pick Plain to print it openly, or hide it in a puzzle. You can hide up to three details.

2

Four puzzle types

A word search spells the message in its leftover letters. A cipher letter gives a code to crack. An acrostic ladder spells it with first letters. A letter maze hides it along the only path. Every answer is checked by code.

3

Match my invitation

Upload your invitation image. The studio picks Paper, Ink and two accent colours, which you can lock or change. Use the image as a banner, corner ornament or faded watermark.

4

Print and sell

Pick 5 x 7, square, Letter or A4 with cut marks. Every invitation keeps a plain RSVP line and a "Stuck? Ask the host" line. Export a 300 DPI PDF or a PNG ZIP, with answer pages at the end.

5

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
