How to use Roll & Race

1

Build your board

Pick a theme, board size, layout, players and dice, then press Generate Kit. Boosts jump forward, slides jump back, and special squares add surprises. Each board is placed by code with no chains or overlaps.

2

The simulation check

Every board is played 2,000 times by the computer. The sidebar shows the average game length with the 5th and 95th percentile. If a board is too short or too long it is rebuilt automatically, up to 20 times.

3

Your own content

Type your own challenge prompts and event cards (add "| +2", "| -1", "| again", "| skip" or "| swap" for an effect). Your text is used first.

4

Make a kit

Choose a KDP trim or the 11 x 8.5 landscape spread for a big board. Book Builder adds the cover, rules, tokens, cards, challenge list and score sheet. Export one PDF or a PNG ZIP; the seed in every footer rebuilds the same kit.

5

Shortcuts

G generate, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
