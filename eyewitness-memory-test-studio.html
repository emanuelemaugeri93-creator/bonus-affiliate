How to use Eyewitness Memory Test

1

Set up the book

Choose how many puzzles and the difficulty. Every puzzle has a study page with a busy scene, a timer page, a separate question page and, from Medium up, a second picture to compare.

2

Fill in My World

Add your town, your shop names and character first names. Use invented names only.

3

Match your cover

Upload the book cover and the From my cover preset takes its colors. Lock or change any swatch.

4

Trim for KDP

Pick the trim and bleed. The blue box is the safe area and the red line is the inside margin. Guides never print.

5

Check and export

Answer view shows the scene with numbered markers and the answers in plain sentences. Export one PDF with page numbers, or use the Print or save as PDF button at the top.

6

Shortcuts

G generate, A answer view, ← → pages, + - zoom, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+P print, H help.
