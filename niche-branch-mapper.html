Niche Branch Mapper

How to use Niche Branch Mapper

Turn one niche into a living map of sellable product ideas.

⚡ In 30 seconds
  1. Type a niche (e.g. dog training) and press Uncover the Goldmine.
  2. Click any gold branch to explode it into concrete product ideas.
  3. Click an idea pill for its mini-pitch, then ★ save or copy it.
  4. Press ⟳ for a fresh set of ideas, or export everything below.
dog trainingsourdough bakingmidlife career changebudget meal prep
🗺️ The 6 branches
Each branch is a monetization format: Ebook & Guide, Printables & Worksheets, KDP Book, Online Course, PLR / Resell Pack, Print on Demand. Click a branch again to collapse it. The counter bottom-left ticks up as you uncover ideas.
★ Favorites & lists
In any idea card press ★ Save to pin it. Click the ★ count bottom-left to review saved ideas, remove them, or export them as Markdown. Copy ideas list copies every visible idea as a checklist.
🔍 Depth & zoom
Use 3 / 4 / 5 bottom-right to set how many ideas each branch explodes into. Use + / − to zoom the map for screenshots and big screens.
📤 Export without tears
  • Export Map as PNG downloads the current view at 2x. If the browser blocks it you get a clear red message — never a blank file.
  • Export ★ .md downloads your saved ideas as a Markdown list.
💡 Best niches to try
Two to four words work best: puppy training, wedding on a budget, indoor herb garden, freelance taxes. Abstract niches work too — the engine adapts (e.g. no coloring book for non-visual topics).
Type a niche first — even two words are enough to strike gold.
