How to use Garden Companion Logic

1

Choose your puzzles

Pick garden sizes from 4 x 4 to 8 x 8, the difficulty levels and the puzzle types, then press Generate Garden Book. Harder puzzles have fewer beds filled in.

2

One solution, always

A solver checks every puzzle and keeps only those with exactly one answer. Foes never touch side by side, and the listed friends must touch at least once.

3

Your own plants and rules

Add custom plants with a simple icon and edit the friend and foe table with lines such as "tomato + sage = foe". Mistakes are listed in red. The table is traditional guidance, not scientific guarantees.

4

Make a book

Choose a KDP trim and bleed. Book Builder adds title, copyright and how-to pages, season sections, a plant guide and answers. Export one PDF or a PNG ZIP. The seed in every footer rebuilds the same book.

5

Shortcuts

G generate, A puzzle/answer view, ← → pages, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, H help.
